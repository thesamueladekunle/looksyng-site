Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Does the subscription include new clothes?

No. The existing-wardrobe subscription plans six looks each month from items you already own. Purchasing or customising new pieces is handled through separate sourcing packages, always with your explicit approval before any spend.

Are laundry and dry-cleaning charges included?

Yes. Laundry and dry-cleaning vendor charges are already included in your subscription — both on the annual and monthly plans. There are no surprise garment-care bills.

Do sourcing package prices include the actual garments?

Package prices cover coordination and service. Actual product costs are approved by you separately before any purchase or commission is made — you will always know what you are spending before we spend it.

Is this only for technology professionals?

No. Technology founders and executives are a natural fit, but the service is designed for any busy professional — executives, entrepreneurs, speakers, frequent event attendees — who values time and consistent preparation.

Is the wardrobe review in person or remote?

We offer both. The preferred approach will be confirmed when you book your consultation, based on your location and what works best for your schedule.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Monthly subscriptions are flexible with no annual commitment. Annual subscriptions require the full upfront payment. Cancellation and renewal terms are confirmed at sign-up. Ask us anything during your consultation.
